Aim

Measure, record, and report

Students collect data on themselves, save it, and write a short laboratory report. The first laboratories use reaction time and a discrimination task. Later meetings extend the same workflow.

Arts and Science

Two options in the modification hour

After the common exercise, Arts students may change the appearance of the display. Science students may add a condition or a measure. Both are graded on a clear prediction and a visible result.

Computing

Repository, not a file drop

After Week 0, each student receives a private GitHub repository. Reports and data files are committed there. See Computing for Codespaces, git, and the AI-disclosure rule.

Assessment

Weights

40% weekly laboratory reports (weeks 1–9; drop the lowest). 20% two in-class critiques (28 October and 2 December). 40% pair project (proposal 18 November; running page 2 December). Reports include a prediction, a result, a limitation, and an AI-use disclosure. Marks are for the argument, not for programming style.
