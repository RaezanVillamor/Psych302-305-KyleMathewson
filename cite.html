Week 6 laboratory

Find, cite, and file a paper

Locate one methods paper, write a complete reference, store a PDF or a stable URL in papers/, and write a half-page annotation. Use the library. Do not accept a chatbot bibliography you have not opened.

Suggested starting points

One paper is enough

  • Donders, F. C. (1868/1969). On the speed of mental processes. Acta Psychologica, 30, 412–431. (translation often reprinted)
  • A short primer on the Big Five or on writing self-report items, assigned in class.

Copy lab-notes/ANNOTATION.md to lab-notes/week06.md. File the source in papers/.

What the annotation must contain

Question, method, limit

Five to eight sentences. Name the dependent measure. State one thing the paper cannot support. A complete reference is required; a hanging-indent polish is not.
